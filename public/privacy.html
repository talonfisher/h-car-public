H-CAR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026
Operated by Talon Fisher Contracting, Courtenay, British Columbia, Canada.
Contact: talonfisher66@gmail.com

H-CAR (Human Condition Analysis & Risk Platform) is a general wellness app. It helps you track patterns in your own wearable data against your personal baseline. H-CAR is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or predict any disease or condition, and it does not replace a healthcare professional.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).

Wearable data. If you connect a Fitbit through Google Fit, we receive daily summaries only: resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), breathing rate, sleep duration, and skin temperature. We do not receive minute-by-minute or raw sensor data.

Information you enter. Symptom logs, morning check-in answers (sleep quality, body feeling, soreness, and context such as alcohol, exertion, illness, or travel), and sensitivity settings. Display settings, such as your light or dark theme choice, are also stored on your account so they follow you across devices.

Health context you choose to share (optional, off by default). If you choose to provide it, we store your biological sex, menopause status, menstrual cycle start dates, pregnancy dates, medications, and elevation for a day. This is used only to adjust what the app expects from your baseline (for example, so a normal cycle change or a high-altitude day is not flagged as abnormal). It is never shown to family members or your emergency contact, never used for advertising, and never sold. You can leave all of it blank and the app works fine.

Emergency contacts. If you add an emergency contact, we store the name, email address, phone number, and relationship you provide, plus an optional secondary contact. We use this only to send alerts as described below.

Location (optional, off by default). If you turn on emergency-only location sharing, the app records your last known location (latitude, longitude, and a street address if available). Location is included in an emergency alert email only when this setting is on.

Automatic elevation detection (optional, off by default). If you turn it on, the app uses your phone location to look up the elevation where you are, so high altitude days are handled without you having to remember. The lookup runs on your phone through Open-Meteo, a free elevation service. Only the elevation in metres is saved to your account. Your latitude and longitude are never stored by H-CAR, never sent to H-CAR servers, and never shared. You can turn this off at any time.

Usage information. We count visits to the site and record sync activity (dates synced, days of data stored) so the service works and we can fix problems.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To build your personal 28-day baseline and compare new daily summaries against it.
  • To show you your trends, status, and history in the app.
  • To send an alert to your emergency contact by email (and by text message if you set that up) when the app detects a sustained multi-signal change that meets its alert rule. Watch-level changes never trigger alerts.
  • To let family members you invite see your status on the Family Dashboard.
  • To respond to support requests and to keep the service running and secure.

3. What We Never Do

  • We never sell your data. Your health information is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never share your data for advertising.
  • We never claim to diagnose you. H-CAR names a change in your data, never a cause.

4. Who We Share Information With

  • Your emergency contact(s), only when an alert fires, and only the information in the alert (your name, the signals that changed, and your last known location if you enabled location sharing).
  • Family members you invite, who can see your status on the Family Dashboard. You can remove a connection at any time.
  • Service providers who run the app: Render (web hosting), Neon (database hosting), Google (Fitbit data sync through Google Fit, only with your consent), and Square (payment processing, only if you ever pay for a subscription; payment card details go directly to Square and are never stored by H-CAR).
  • As required by law, if we receive a lawful order from a court or regulator.

5. Where Your Data Lives

Data is hosted in the United States (Render and Neon, US East). If you are in Canada, your information is stored outside Canada and may be subject to the laws of the United States. We take reasonable steps to keep it secure, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted stored credentials, and hashed passwords.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your account and wearable summaries while your account is active. If you delete your account or ask us to delete your data, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records for legal or accounting reasons.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can disconnect your Fitbit at any time from the app. New data stops syncing, and existing data stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • You can change or remove your emergency contacts at any time.
  • You can turn location sharing off at any time.
  • You can turn automatic elevation detection off at any time from the About You section in Profile.
  • You can download your data from the app (Settings, Data Export).
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account by emailing talonfisher66@gmail.com.

Residents of British Columbia have rights under the BC Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and federal privacy law (PIPEDA) may also apply. To make a privacy request or complaint, contact us at the email above. You may also contact the BC Information and Privacy Commissioner if you are not satisfied with our response.

8. Children

H-CAR is for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Cookies and Tracking

H-CAR uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not share usage data with advertisers.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your information is used, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you by email before the change takes effect.
