What is H-CAR?

H-CAR learns your personal baseline from your Fitbit and flags sustained changes over days. It names a change, never a cause. It never tells you why something changed, only that it did.

How it works:

  • Wear your Fitbit for 28 days to build your personal baseline
  • H-CAR tracks 6 signals: resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, breathing rate, sleep, skin temperature
  • When multiple signals move away from your baseline, you get a flag
  • Serious flags can alert a person you trust

2. What H-CAR does

H-CAR is an experimental general wellness tool. It learns your personal baseline from a consumer Fitbit and flags sustained multi-signal change over days. It names a change, never a cause. A flag means your numbers moved away from your own baseline. It does not mean you are sick. No flag does not mean you are healthy.

3. What H-CAR cannot detect

H-CAR CANNOT detect sudden cardiac arrest, stroke, heart attack, severe allergic reaction, sudden collapse, or falls. H-CAR does not monitor you in real time. If you have a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. Do not wait for H-CAR to flag anything.

4. Use at your own risk

H-CAR is provided as-is without warranties of any kind. H-CAR may produce false alarms or miss real changes. Fitbit sensors provide estimates, not clinical measurements. You use H-CAR at your own risk.

6. Your data
