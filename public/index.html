What is H-CAR?

H-CAR learns your personal baseline from your Fitbit and flags sustained changes over days. It names a change, never a cause. It never tells you why something changed, only that it did.

How it works:

  • Wear your Fitbit for 28 days to build your personal baseline
  • H-CAR tracks 6 signals: resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, breathing rate, sleep, skin temperature
  • When multiple signals move away from your baseline, you get a flag
  • Serious flags can alert a person you trust

H-CAR does NOT provide medical advice. If you feel unwell, seek professional medical care immediately, whether or not H-CAR has flagged anything.

2. What H-CAR does

H-CAR is an experimental general wellness tool. It learns your personal baseline from a consumer Fitbit and flags sustained multi-signal change over days. It names a change, never a cause. A flag means your numbers moved away from your own baseline. It does not mean you are sick. No flag does not mean you are healthy.

3. What H-CAR cannot detect

H-CAR CANNOT detect sudden cardiac arrest, stroke, heart attack, severe allergic reaction, sudden collapse, or falls. H-CAR does not monitor you in real time. If you have a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. Do not wait for H-CAR to flag anything.

4. Use at your own risk

H-CAR is provided as-is without warranties of any kind. H-CAR may produce false alarms or miss real changes. Fitbit sensors provide estimates, not clinical measurements. You use H-CAR at your own risk.

5. Limitation of liability

Total liability is capped at the greater of 12 months of fees paid or $60 CAD. This excludes indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.

6. Your data

H-CAR uses edge-first processing. Your baseline is calculated on your device. Only flag summaries and daily heartbeats reach the server. Your raw Fitbit data stays on your device when possible. Your data is never sold. You can delete your account and data at any time.

7. Subscription terms

$5.00 CAD per month, auto-renewing. Cancel anytime, effective at the end of your paid period. No refunds for partial months. Payment is processed by Square. Your card details are tokenized by Square and never touch our servers.

8. Research use of de-identified data

H-CAR is being validated in a trial. Your de-identified health summaries (daily signal values, baseline statistics, and flag counts, with your name, email, and account details removed) may be used for research to validate whether H-CAR's detector works. De-identified data may be shared with research partners and published in aggregate form. Your identifiable data is never sold and never shared for research. You can withdraw from the trial at any time by cancelling your subscription and deleting your account; data already de-identified and pooled cannot be pulled back out.
